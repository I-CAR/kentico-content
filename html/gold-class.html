What Is Gold Class?

The I‑CAR Gold Class program helps auto body repairers and insurers stay ahead with role-relevant education on evolving vehicle systems. More than 10,000 businesses have earned this recognition, setting the benchmark for industry excellence. When you commit to being Gold Class, you prioritize complete, safe, and quality repairs while supporting original equipment manufacturer standards that improve operational efficiency and build customer confidence.

Gold Class for Collision Repair

Collision repair facilities earn Gold Class recognition through I‑CAR’s Professional Development Program. It includes specialized body shop training and certification that equips your team with the knowledge and skills needed to work on today’s vehicles.

Benefits of Being a Gold Class Shop

  • Enjoy a 20% discount on I‑CAR training courses
  • Stand out as a top-tier collision repair provider
  • Enhance your team with specialized collision training
  • Able to enroll in collision repair and I‑CAR Academy subscription programs

Gold Class for Insurance

Insurance companies achieve Gold Class status through I‑CAR’s Professional Development Program for Auto Physical Damage Appraisers (APDAs). This specialized insurance training helps to ensure accurate estimates and proper repair procedures.

Benefits of Being a Gold Class Insurer

  • Enjoy a 20% discount on professional development courses
  • Stand out as a premier vehicle insurance provider
  • Access specialized training for your team
