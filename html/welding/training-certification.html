Welding Training & Certification

Modern vehicles rely on advanced materials and precise welding for structural integrity and safety. As a certified welding technician, you ensure every weld meets OEM specifications after collision repairs.

Responsibilities

  • Applying specialized welding knowledge that supports every role in the collision repair facility
  • Performing welds that meet OEM-approved procedures and safety standards
  • Understanding how welding integrates with structural, non-structural, and refinishing processes
  • Staying current with evolving welding technologies and repair requirements

Path to Certification

Getting Certified

  • Complete online and hands-on course prerequisites
  • Learn how to adjust and tune welders
  • Maintain recertification every 3 years

Recertification Process

  • Involves only performance tests
  • Requires welders to perform the welds without assistance
  • Students should already be familiar with how to adjust and tune welding machines
