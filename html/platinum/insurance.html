Individuals who achieve Platinum recognition can benefit from enhanced career opportunities and have the ability to:

  • Contribute to accurate claims process
  • Improve overall operational efficiencies in repair process
  • Understand damage analysis procedures on current vehicle technology
  • Interact and communicate effectively with customers about vehicle repairs

Achieving Platinum Recognition

To achieve Platinum designation, an individual must complete all ProLevel 1® requirements for his or her role. Upon completing these requirements a Platinum certificate can be downloaded, and its date becomes the individual’s annual renewal date to maintain Platinum.

To maintain Platinum designation, the individual must complete all ProLevel 2® requirements by the next annual renewal date. The individual must then complete all ProLevel 3® requirements by the following annual renewal date. After achieving ProLevel 3, the individual must complete six annual training course credits by the annual renewal date.

If Platinum status has expired, the individual will still retain his or her training records and credit earned for any ProLevel previously completed. Platinum status can be regained by achieving the next ProLevel being worked towards. This rule applies no matter how long the gap in training was, and even if the individual goes to another business.
