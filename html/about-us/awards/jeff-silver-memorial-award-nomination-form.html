Jeff Silver Platinum Memorial Award

Recognizing Achievement in the Collision Repair Industry

First presented in 2009, the Jeff Silver Platinum Individual Award honors Jeff Silver, a visionary leader known for his commitment to technical training and excellence within the collision repair industry.

Platinum Individual Nomination Form for 2025 Performance Year

If nominating a Platinum individual, please provide the nominee's information below. If submitting on their behalf, include your contact information in the submitter section.

View Previous I‑CAR Award Recipients

Purpose

This award recognizes individuals who demonstrate a commitment to continuous learning, professional growth, and advancing industry standards through ongoing training and development.

Eligibility

This award will be bestowed upon a select individual that meets the following requirements:

  • Holds a long term (defined as at least five consecutive years) I‑CAR Platinum Individual designation
  • Trains beyond the minimum requirement to maintain the designation
  • Available for a member of any industry segment that meets criteria

Please note all current I‑CAR employees are not eligible to earn this award. This includes I‑CAR full-time and part-time instructors.

Nominating Procedure

Complete this form and submit it prior to July 31, 2026. For questions, email i‑car.events.dept@i-car.com.

Presentation of Award

The award will be presented during 2026 SEMA Week in Las Vegas, on Tuesday, November 3, at the Collision Industry Red Carpet Awards.
