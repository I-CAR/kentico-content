Recognizing Achievement in the Collision Repair Industry

Platinum™ Individual Nomination Form for Performance Year 2023

Purpose

Mr. Jeff Silver was one of the forefathers of what today is the industry’s standard for training excellence: the I‑CAR® Gold Class® designation for shops and the Platinum recognition for individuals. In his service as I‑CAR’s CEO from 1985 through 1996, he guided I‑CAR through precarious times to become the industry’s leading provider of educational programming and technical services. Later in his life, he maintained Platinum recognition when he and his wife Jeanne operated an Illinois CARSTAR shop that reached its 25-year Gold Class anniversary. He is remembered with this award that honors individuals who, as he did, embody a true passion for training and professional growth.

Eligibility

This award will be bestowed upon a select individual that meets the following requirements:

  • Holds a long term (defined as at least five consecutive years) I‑CAR Platinum Individual designation
  • Trains beyond the minimum requirement to maintain the designation
  • Available for a member of any industry segment that meets criteria

Please note all current I‑CAR employees are not eligible to earn this award. This includes I‑CAR full-time and part-time instructors.

Nominating Procedure

Complete this form and submit it prior to June 30, 2024. For questions, email i-car.events.dept@i-car.com.

Judging

I‑CAR staff, I‑CAR Executive Committee, I‑CAR Board of Directors, and/or other collision repair industry representatives will select the recipient.

Presentation of Award

The award will be presented during 2024 SEMA Week in Las Vegas, on Tuesday, November 5, at the Collision Industry Red Carpet Ceremony.
