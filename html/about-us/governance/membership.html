Make Your Impact on the Collision Repair Industry

As an I‑CAR Member, you are part of a community of over 45,000 collision repair industry professionals united around the common goal of providing complete, safe, and quality repairs for millions of drivers.

Who are I‑CAR Members?

The Membership community includes all facets of the collision industry, such as vehicle manufacturers, repairers, insurers, technicians, equipment manufacturers, and information suppliers.
