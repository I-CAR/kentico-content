ADAS Education

As vehicles become more advanced, collision repair professionals face new challenges, especially with ADAS now standard in many models. Understanding how these systems work and how to properly diagnose, repair, and calibrate them is no longer optional.

I‑CAR’s ADAS training equips technicians with the knowledge and hands-on skills needed for safe, complete, and OEM-compliant repairs. Explore our courses to stay ahead of evolving technology and ensure every vehicle you touch is road‑ready.
