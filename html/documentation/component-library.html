Talent Programming Initiative

$20.9M OVER 3 YEARS

I-CAR is committed to attracting, training, and retaining technicians who will fuel the future of the industry. Through our U.S. Department of Labor-approved National Registered Apprenticeship program, we're expanding access to structured, earn-while-you-learn career pathways. We're also collaborating to better position the industry, implementing shop-approved training solutions, and providing schools with next-level programming to support tomorrow's technicians.

Consumer Branding Awareness

$1.8M OVER 18 MONTHS

I-CAR is strengthening its consumer brand to promote safety and build trust in collision repair. Through our “Count on I-CAR” message, a refreshed brand, consumer education on the value of Gold Class, and the Gold Class Shop Locator at GoldClass.I-CAR.com, we're helping consumers make informed decisions while equipping Gold Class shops with marketing tools to promote their commitment to safe, quality repairs.

Advanced Learning Effectiveness

$1.1M OVER 1.5 YEARS

Through continued research, I-CAR has reimagined course delivery to enhance learning and accessibility. We're introducing more engaging, flexible experiences through interactive and adaptive online learning, paired with a more hands-on approach using Mixed Reality to better prepare technicians for real-world application.

Section Headline

Our core beliefs and values guide how we work together and shape the impact we make every day.

Collaboration

I‑CAR is dedicated to fostering a team environment while understanding and acting on the needs of the Inter-Industry.

This commitment is demonstrated by empowering and appreciating engagement and teamwork, listening to and being guided by the Voice-of-the-Industry, serving with integrity as a neutral forum for all parties, encouraging participation and involvement at all levels, supporting a respectful culture, and working cooperatively both internally and externally.

Relevance

I‑CAR is dedicated to continually enhancing the value delivered to the Inter-Industry in curriculum, courses and services.

This commitment is demonstrated by listening and acting appropriately while ensuring I‑CAR's sustainability, setting priorities responsive to customer needs and industry innovations, and being an industry-minded enterprise focused on advancing the knowledge of the industry, its companies, the individual who serve in it and those who are served by it.

Expertise

I‑CAR is dedicated to being a respected resource for the Inter-Industry.

This commitment is demonstrated by maintaining leadership and current knowledge in education, training, vehicle and repair technologies, and industry best practices; providing expert insight to the Inter-Industry; staying well informed about current and anticipated conditions; and retaining skilled professionals recognized as subject matter experts in their fields.

Excellence

I‑CAR is dedicated to being the best we can possibly be for the ultimate benefit of the industry we serve, as well as our volunteers, instructors and staff.

This commitment is demonstrated by being accountable and holding each other accountable in a team-spirited manner, continually striving to be extraordinary in service and operation, exhibiting a sense of urgency and a bias for action, having a strong work ethic and being disciplined about how we approach our work, and always possessing a conscience about being the best we can possibly be.

Integrity

I‑CAR is dedicated to being trustworthy.

This commitment is demonstrated by delivering on promises; being ethical, honest, fair, and sincere in communications and behavior; and being transparent in our decision-making.
