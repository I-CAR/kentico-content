What Is Platinum?

The Platinum designation is awarded to individuals who complete I‑CAR’s detailed, role-specific collision repair training. Whether you’re an automotive technician, estimator, or appraiser, earning Platinum shows you understand the latest vehicle systems and repair procedures. It also helps your business earn or maintain I‑CAR’s Gold Class recognition.

Why Go Platinum?

Achieving Platinum sets you apart as a leader in auto body repair—demonstrating your expertise and commitment to excellence. As a Platinum professional, you will:

  • Stand out with in-depth knowledge of modern vehicle repairs
  • Become a key asset in your shop with trusted, current skills
  • Show your commitment to ongoing learning and career growth
  • Earn national recognition as a top performer in the industry
  • Position yourself for better career opportunities and advancement
  • Boost your confidence and credibility in your work
  • Receive a 20% discount on all I‑CAR training

Platinum Training Standards

Platinum training is structured across three levels: ProLevel 1, 2, and 3.
To maintain your Platinum status, you must complete six training course credits each year by your renewal date.

Falling behind puts your recognition at risk—but your progress isn’t lost. Completed ProLevel credits are retained, and any missed training can be completed to reinstate Platinum.

What’s New

Appraiser Platinum Changes

Starting August 2026, Auto Physical Damage Appraisers (APDAs) will need to complete ProLevel 1, 2, and 3 training to become Platinum. This auto collision training ensures APDAs are prepared for the future of claims processing and advanced vehicle technology, staying aligned with industry standards.
