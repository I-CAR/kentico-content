Industry Reinvestment

I‑CAR's Industry Impact Fund is a strategic reserve component governed by the I‑CAR Board for the industry's benefit. These reinvestments have contributed to I‑CAR's strong financial position, enabling the organization to continue providing valuable training and services to the collision repair industry. I‑CAR is committed to leading the industry forward with excellence in training, technical information, and collaboration in a dynamic environment impacted by increasing repair complexity and challenges recruiting technicians.

Re-investments to Support the Industry

A healthy reserve with Board oversight, funded by ongoing retained earnings, is vital to I‑CAR's ongoing operational sustainability while enabling reinvestment in strategic initiatives responsive to industry needs. I‑CAR reserve levels are governed within not-for-profit best practices for organizations with similar scales of operations and service levels. Reserves are there to help weather emergencies or recessionary pressures, provide for necessary capital reinvestments and to support strategic initiatives that directly support industry needs, allowing I‑CAR to self-fund versus incurring debt. I‑CAR has diligently re-invested in strategic initiatives that further industry training outcomes, strategically expand services driven by industry voice, and annual support for CREF, all geared to create industry benefits that align with I‑CAR's vision and mission.
