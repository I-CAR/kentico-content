I-CAR Leadership

I-CAR's Leadership Team advances initiatives that bring our mission to life by delivering the education, knowledge, and solutions needed for complete, safe, quality repairs that protect consumers.

With a deep understanding of the collision repair ecosystem, our leaders collaborate to guide strategy, partnerships, and innovation across the inter-industry. Focused on talent development, training accessibility, and technological readiness, they ensure I-CAR remains responsive to the evolving needs of repairers, insurers, OEMs, educators, and suppliers. This work strengthens both the industry and the communities it serves.
