Become a Board Member

I-CAR is accepting applications from qualified candidates interested in serving on its Board of Directors. This is a meaningful opportunity to step into a leadership role that helps shape the strategic direction, governance, and long-term impact of I-CAR and the collision repair industry.

Applications are open to eligible representatives across several industry segments and will be reviewed in accordance with I-CAR’s established nominating and election process.

Terms and Expectations

Each term lasts three years, and members may serve up to two consecutive terms. Board members are expected to attend four meetings annually.

  • Winter - Virtual
  • Spring - In Person
  • Summer - In Person
  • Fall - In Person
