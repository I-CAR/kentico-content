Welding

Welding is an essential skill for structural integrity and occupant safety after a collision. Improper welds can lead to repair failures and safety risks. That’s why OEM network and Direct Repair Program requirements demand certified welding technicians.

  • Certification

Different I‑CAR Welding Certifications

  • Steel GMA Welding

    Focused on Gas Metal Arc (MIG) welding for steel components

  • Steel Sectioning

    Covers proper sectioning and joining techniques for structural steel repairs

  • Aluminum GMA (MIG) Welding

    Designed for aluminum-intensive vehicles and OEM-approved MIG welding processes
