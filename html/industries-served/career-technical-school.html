Professional Development Program - Education Edition (PDP‑EE)

I‑CAR PDP‑EE program was developed to equip Career & Technical Education schools and educators with collision repair curriculum for their students. As vehicle technology and repair processes advanced, our entry-level curriculum, has evolved to keep pace, ensuring technicians are equipped with the latest skills and knowledge. I‑CAR Academy for schools will completely replace PDP‑EE by 2027.

For detailed transition timelines and milestones, please e-mail academy.schools@i-car.com.
