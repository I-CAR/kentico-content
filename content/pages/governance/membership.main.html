I‑CAR Membership

As an I‑CAR Member, you are part of a community of over 45,000 collision repair industry professionals united around the common goal of providing complete, safe, and quality repairs for millions of drivers.

Who are I‑CAR Members?

The Membership community includes all facets of the collision industry, such as vehicle manufacturers, repairers, insurers, technicians, equipment manufacturers, and information suppliers.

Gold Class Collision Repair Businesses

Each Gold Class repair facility has a Designated Membership Representative

Platinum Technicians

Every Platinum technician is an I‑CAR Member

Career and Technical Schools

Each qualifying Career and Technical School has a Designated Membership Representative

I‑CAR State or Committee Chairs

Every volunteer Committee Chair is an I‑CAR Member

Sustaining Partners

Every Sustaining Partner organization has a Designated Membership Representative

Membership Pricing

Member dues must be received, or postmarked no later than December 31 of the prior year to be eligible to vote in the following membership term. No such member status will be effective until member dues are received.

Independent Member – If you are an independent (single location) collision repair business with annual sales of less than $3 million, a career technical school (without an active licensing subscription), an individual unemployed or retired individual, employee in a business with annual sales less than or equal to $3 million or employed by a business already with Regular Member status.
Annual Dues: $500.00

Organizational Member – If you are a multi-shop operator with two or more locations, insurer, vehicle manufacturer, tool/equipment/miscellaneous supplier, and related industry organization or association.
Annual Dues: $1,000.00
