Interested in Joining a Council?

We welcome industry professionals who are passionate about advancing collision repair to explore opportunities to join an I‑CAR council. Councils provide a unique platform to share expertise, offer guidance, and influence initiatives that strengthen the industry.

Membership is by invitation or selection, and participation may be limited to ensure each council has the right mix of experience and perspectives. If you are interested, we encourage you to express your interest. Your insights and ideas could help shape the future of collision repair education and innovation.
