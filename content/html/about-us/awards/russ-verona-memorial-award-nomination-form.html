Recognizing Achievement in the Collision Repair Industry

Collision Repair Shop Nomination Form for Performance Year 2023

Purpose

Russ Verona was a true pioneer of professionalism in the collision repair industry. His dedication to the industry was apparent in all that he accomplished, his support of industry associations, and the fact that he owned and operated the first ever I‑CAR® Gold Class® designated business. Russ was a mentor, a friend, and a role model for the collision repair industry. The Russ Verona Memorial Award honors his memory and contributions to the industry.

Eligibility

This award will be bestowed upon a select collision repair shop that meets the following requirements:

  • Holds a long term (defined as at least five consecutive years) I‑CAR Gold Class designation
  • Actively promotes the Gold Class designation, training, and a positive image of the collision industry (locally or on a broader scale)

Nominating Procedure

Complete this form and submit it prior to June 30, 2024. For questions, email i-car.events.dept@i-car.com.

Judging

I‑CAR staff, I‑CAR Executive Committee, I‑CAR Board of Directors, and/or other collision repair industry representatives will select the recipient.

Presentation of Award

The award will be presented during 2024 SEMA Week in Las Vegas, on Tuesday, November 5, at the Collision Industry Red Carpet Ceremony.
