Platinum Recognition

  • Effective date: January 10, 2022
    A new report which identifies courses required for technicians to achieve or maintain Platinum is now available under the “Gold Class® Summary” menu in myI‑CAR®. This report may also be exported to csv format.
  • Effective date: July 13, 2021
    If a technician’s Platinum recognition expires, gap training for ProLevel 1, ProLevel 2 and ProLevel 3 will be required to fulfill knowledge and skills gaps before achieving Platinum Recognition.
  • Effective date: April 26, 2021
    If a Platinum individual expires with only a certification renewal requirement remaining, gap training will update upon expiration of Platinum recognition.
  • Effective date: January 25, 2021
    The All Training Report accurately reflects Platinum renewal dates within a current Gold Class renewal cycle.
  • Effective date: July 13, 2020
    Platinum™ Recognition renews on an individual’s Platinum renewal date even when requirements for renewal are met prior to that date.
  • Effective date: April 20, 2020
    Platinum discounts for new course enrollments will automatically apply in the shopping cart for students who achieved Platinum Recognition.
  • Effective date: March 21, 2020
    Individuals can now view progress toward Platinum™ recognition. The snapshot under My Training on a student level helps to identify training milestones to help plan toward achieving individual and/or organizational goals.
  • Effective date: March 21, 2020
    If a record for an individual that achieved Platinum did not migrate correctly, the Gold Class status report was affected. This affected a small population but it was identified and addressed in our recent release to ensure student and location-level records are reflecting accurate data.
  • Effective date: January 22, 2020
    If a technician’s Platinum status expired, his/her ProLevel progress was reverting back to ProLevel 1. The system was updated to keep a Platinum individual ProLevel progress even if the Platinum recognition expires allowing an individual to start their journey to progress from where he/she ended.

Reminder: If a Platinum technician does not renew any certificates and/or progress through gap training, his/her Platinum status will not renew.
