Russ Verona Memorial Award

Presented to a select U.S. collision repair business that has held the I‑CAR Gold Class designation for at least five consecutive years, this award honors businesses that actively promote the Gold Class designation and training and that reinforce the positive image of the collision repair industry.

Past Winners (year denotes when presented the award):

  • 2006 – East Rockford Collision Center, Nick Verona
  • 2007 – Florida Auto Body, Jimmy and Julia Suppe
  • 2008 – Zara’s Collision Center, Brad Zara
  • 2009 – North Kitsap Auto Rebuild, Jim Sund
  • 2011 – Grossman CARSTAR
  • 2012 – Gustafson Bros, John Gustafson
  • 2013 – Oakland Auto Body, Ron Vincenzi
  • 2014 – Master Auto Body, Mike Minutillo
  • 2015 – Mike Loy’s Collision Inc., Mike Loy
  • 2016 – Precision Unlimited CarCare, Brad Czerkies
  • 2017 – Car Crafters, Jim Guthrie
  • 2018 – CARSTAR Mundelein, Jeanne and Jeff Silver
  • 2019 – Dalton Collision, Jerry Dalton
  • 2020 – Hayes Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram, Ted Hayes
  • 2021 – Schaefer Autobody Center, Webster Groves, MO, Scott Schaefer
  • 2022 – Eveland Brothers Collision Repair Inc, Bill Eveland
  • 2023 – Clinton Body Shop, John Mosley
