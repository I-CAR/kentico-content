Vehicle technology for active safety systems and Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) continues to progress rapidly. While ADAS components are relatively small, they can significantly impact the cost of collision repairs. I‑CAR® continues to work with automakers and industry groups to develop repair protocols and secure the information needed to help ensure that repair procedures for new vehicles are available as quickly as possible.

Mark Bochenek, I‑CAR Principal, OEM Business Development, has authored an informative new I‑CAR white paper entitled Designing Vehicles with Repairability in Mind that provides a wealth of useful information.
