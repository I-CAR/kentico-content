The Sustaining Partner Program was developed to champion the various initiatives that drive I‑CAR's vision forward - that every person in the collision repair industry has the information, knowledge and skills required to perform complete, safe and quality repairs. Collision repair professionals and students benefit from greater access to quality education and resources.

Sustaining Partners play a vital role in strengthening our industry as we focus on the sustainability of professionals and organizations who are facing a Technical Tsunami, talent shortage, and growing repair complexity. Together, we're not only steering the course of individual careers, we are collectively charting a path toward a thriving collision repair industry.
