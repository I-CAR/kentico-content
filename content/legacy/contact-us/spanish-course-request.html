Students may choose “Spanish – Latin American” as his/her learning language preference under “My Profile” when logged into myI‑CAR®.

When an I‑CAR English language course(s) that has a Spanish equivalent available is selected for enrollment, the Spanish version will automatically be added to the student’s online learning experience.

Click here for a list of courses available in Spanish.

Training Managers can identify students at their location who selected “Spanish – Latin American” as his/her learning language preference, by an icon with "LA" next to the student’s name.

Follow these instructions on how to update the learning language preference on your myI‑CAR account.
