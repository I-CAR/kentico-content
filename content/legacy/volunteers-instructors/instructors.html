I‑CAR’s instructors teach up-to-date collision and mechanical repair methods to tens of thousands of people each year at locations throughout the U.S. They are provided with professionally developed classroom materials, technical information and coaching in presentation skills. In addition, I‑CAR welding instructors train and coach technicians in the latest welding methods and techniques with hands-on performance activities in their own workplaces using their own equipment for the most practical learning experience possible.

If you are interested in becoming an instructor with I‑CAR, please visit our career portal to see open positions.

Active I‑CAR Instructors please login to myI‑CAR for courses and other materials.
