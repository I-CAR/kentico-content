High-Voltage Safety

Electric and hybrid vehicles introduce unique safety risks due to high-voltage systems. Use these resources to learn about proper safety procedures before starting repairs.

Common EV and Hybrid Safety Questions

A Plug-In Hybrid Electric Vehicle (PHEV) uses both an electric motor and a gas engine, and you can recharge the battery by plugging it in.

A Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV) also combines electric and gas power, but it recharges through driving, no plug-in required.

A Battery Electric Vehicle (BEV) runs fully on electricity and uses a high-voltage battery as its only power source.

An Extended-Range Electric Vehicle (EREV) drives mainly on electric power but includes a small gas engine that generates electricity when needed.
