Travel to the Technical Center

The I‑CAR Chicago Technical Center in Vernon Hills, Illinois, is about 30 miles north of downtown Chicago and close to O’Hare International Airport. The facility expands I‑CAR’s technical resources with state-of-the-art labs for ADAS, EVs, and Welding, along with modern classrooms and a Learning Design Studio. Built by I‑CAR for the industry, it provides an ideal setting for training, collaboration, and advancing collision repair innovation.
